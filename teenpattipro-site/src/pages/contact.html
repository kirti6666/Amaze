Website questions

For questions about this website or its guides, email {{contactEmail}}. We usually reply within two working days.

Account, deposit or game issues

For anything related to your account inside the app, the quickest route is the Service or Email button in the app’s lobby, so the support team can see your account details securely.

Stay safe

We will never ask for your password, OTP, UPI PIN or card details by phone, WhatsApp or email. Ignore anyone who does and report them.
