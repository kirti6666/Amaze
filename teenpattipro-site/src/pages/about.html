This website is the official home of the {{siteName}} Android app. Here you can download the latest APK safely and learn the rules of every game in the app.

What we publish

  • Download and install help for the official APK, kept up to date with each release.
  • Game guides that explain the rules of Teen Patti, Dragon vs Tiger, Andar Bahar and other games in plain language.
  • Responsible gaming information, because we want play to stay fun.

How we write our guides

Our editorial team checks every rule against how the games actually play in the app, works out probabilities from first principles, and updates articles when anything changes. We never claim that a game can be beaten with tricks or that you are guaranteed to win.
