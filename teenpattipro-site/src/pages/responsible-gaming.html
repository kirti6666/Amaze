Games like Teen Patti, Dragon vs Tiger and Andar Bahar should be entertainment, never a way to make money or solve financial problems. Games that involve money carry financial risk and can be addictive. Please play responsibly and at your own risk.

18+ only

{{siteName}} is intended only for adults aged 18 and over. If you are a parent, use your phone’s parental controls (such as Google Family Link) to block apps and websites that are not suitable for children.

Check the law where you live

Laws on online games involving money differ from country to country and, in India, from state to state — and they change. Some jurisdictions restrict or completely prohibit online money games and their promotion. It is your responsibility to make sure that downloading and using the app is legal where you are. If it isn’t, please don’t download it.

Simple rules for safer play

  • Set a budget before you start, and only use money you can afford to lose.
  • Set a time limit and use a phone timer or Digital Wellbeing to enforce it.
  • Never chase losses by betting more to win back what you lost.
  • Don’t play when stressed, upset, tired or after drinking.
  • Never borrow money or use money meant for bills, rent or family to play.
  • Take breaks and keep up your other hobbies and social life.

Warning signs

It may be time to stop or get help if you:

  • spend more money or time than you planned, again and again;
  • hide your playing from family or friends;
  • feel anxious, irritable or low when you are not playing;
  • borrow money, sell belongings or miss responsibilities because of gaming.

Get help

Talking to someone helps. In India you can call the free government mental-health helpline Tele-MANAS: 14416 (or 1-800-891-4416), available 24/7 in many languages. Outside India, organisations such as Gambling Therapy offer free, confidential support.

You can also use your phone’s app-timer settings to limit or block gaming apps, or simply uninstall the app.
